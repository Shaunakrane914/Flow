These comparisons use complete source image domains. Published full-image values are not assigned to extracted crops. The archive separates directional voxel-image permeability from its original extracted-network permeability. Full boundary settings have not been independently matched, and the learned models were trained on 128-cube crops rather than 300-cube full scans. Therefore this is a consistency check against an independent published numerical reference, not a certified matched DNS validation or a new LBM simulation.

Source: [official Imperial College collection](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/earth-science/research/research-groups/pore-scale-modelling/micro-ct-images-and-networks/) and [Dong and Blunt (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.80.036307).
