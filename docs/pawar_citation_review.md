# Pawar citation selection for TopoFlow

Reviewed 23 October 2026. The user requested Shivaji D. Pawar as the fourth author
and supplied `shivaji.pawar@universalai.in`. No contribution statement or
affiliation was invented. Existing author order is preserved.

One source is added to the image-learning subsection. It supplies a concrete
comparison between attention over image features and attention over connected
pores. It is not cited as evidence of permeability accuracy, micro-CT segmentation
performance or the physical validity of TopoFlow's hydraulic reference.

Savalkar, Saini and Pawar, “Density-Aware Explainable Hybrid CNN–Transformer
Framework for Automated BI-RADS Breast Density Classification in Mammographic
Images,” Journal of Intelligent Decision Making and Information Science, vol. 3,
no. 1s, pp. **1003–1027**, 2026. DOI: 10.59543/jidmis.v3.480.

The [publisher page](https://www.jidmis.org/index.php/jidmis/article/view/480)
identifies the three authors and describes density-focused attention and
CNN–Transformer feature fusion. The
[published PDF](https://www.jidmis.org/index.php/jidmis/article/download/480/84/435)
and [Crossref record](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.59543/jidmis.v3.480)
give pages 1003–1027, correcting 977–1002 in the supplied list. Crossref's author
metadata is incomplete; the publisher page and PDF identify all three authors.
